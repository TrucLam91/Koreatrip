Gwangjang Market (광장시장)
Daily 8 AM–11 PM · Some stalls open later
Must Order: Bindaetteok + Mayak Gimbap
₩5,000–8,000 per item · €4–6
Seoul's oldest and most authentic market. Bindaetteok (mung bean pancakes, fried crispy at live stalls) and mayak gimbap (bite-sized seaweed rice rolls with sesame) are the two iconic dishes. Eat at the women's stall tables with makgeolli (rice wine).
🎵 TikTok Viral
🍗
Han River Chimaek (치맥)
Order from local fried chicken shops or convenience stores · Available 24/7
Must Order: Fried chicken + Maekju (beer)
₩15,000–25,000 for 2 pax · €11–18
Korea's most iconic couple date activity. Buy half-and-half fried chicken (반반), 2 cans of beer, and go sit on the Han River grass with a blue mat (buy at convenience store ₩2,000). Yeouido Hangang Park is the most popular spot.
🎵 TikTok Viral
🌮
Myeongdong Night Market Street Food
Every evening 5 PM–11 PM
Must Order: Tornado Potato + Grilled Cheese
₩3,000–8,000 per item · €2–6
The nightly outdoor food market on Myeongdong's main pedestrian street. Try: tornado potato (₩4,000), cheese lobster skewer (₩8,000), hotteok (sweet pancake ₩2,000), tteokbokki (spicy rice cakes ₩4,000). Very TikTok-friendly visuals.
🎵 TikTok Viral
🔥
Korean BBQ AYCE (삼겹살)
Open daily 11 AM–midnight (most restaurants)
Must Order: Samgyeopsal + Soju
₩13,000–18,000 AYCE per person · €10–13
All-you-can-eat Korean BBQ is mandatory in Seoul. Grill samgyeopsal (pork belly) on a table grill, wrap in lettuce with garlic, kimchi and ssamjang paste, wash down with soju or maekju. Try: Mapo Galmaegi (Hongdae area) or any street restaurant near your hotel.
🎵 TikTok Viral
🏪
Convenience Store Mukbang
Open 24/7
Must Order: Cup Ramen + Triangle Gimbap + Hotdog
₩800–3,000 per item · €1–2
A Korean cultural institution — sitting outside CU or GS25 at a plastic table at midnight with cup ramyeon, triangle gimbap and a beer. The hot dog on a stick (₩1,200) from inside the store is oddly incredible. A genuine TikTok trend globally.
🎵 TikTok Viral
🧊
Bingsu (팥빙수) at Sulbing
Open daily 10 AM–10 PM
Must Order: Strawberry / Injeolmi Bingsu
₩7,000–12,000 · €5–9
Korean shaved ice dessert. Mountains of soft shaved milk ice topped with sweet red beans, mochi, condensed milk and fresh fruit. Perfect for hot June weather. Sulbing chain is reliable and everywhere. The injeolmi (roasted soy powder) version is the most Korean.

Seongsan Ilchulbong (성산일출봉)
UNESCO Sunrise Crater
🔥 TikTok Trending
🕐 6 AM – 8 PM · Sunrise: ~5:30 AM (June)💰 ₩5,000 (~€3.5)
A 182m tuff cone crater that emerges from the sea — one of Korea's most iconic images and a TikTok staple. The sunrise from the crater rim is breathtaking. Only accessible before 8 AM for the sunrise experience.
🔥 TikTok ViralUNESCOSunriseIconicMust-Do
💡 Stay at Seongsan the night before. Arrive at crater entrance 5 AM for the 20-min climb before sunrise. Pack a jacket (windy at top).
🏝
Udo Island Day Trip (우도)
Turquoise Sea Island Getaway
🔥 TikTok Trending
🕐 Ferries 7:30 AM – 5:30 PM💰 Ferry ₩3,000 + scooter rental ₩30,000/day
Small island off Seongsan accessible by 15-min ferry. Famous for peanut ice cream (땅콩 아이스크림), turquoise Jongdal Coast, and scooter rides along the coastline. One of the most TikTok-worthy day trips in Korea.
🔥 TikTok ViralIslandScooterPeanut Ice CreamCouple
💡 Rent a 50cc scooter on the island and circle it in 2–3 hours. Stop for peanut ice cream at every stall you see.
🍵
O'Sulloc Tea Museum
Matcha Paradise · Instagram Famous
🔥 TikTok Trending
🕐 9 AM – 7 PM daily💰 Free entry, ₩5,500 soft serve
Nestled among rolling green tea fields, this museum/café is a visual and culinary dream. Free to visit the fields and museum. The green tea soft serve is the star item. The surrounding Innisfree Jeju House has free green tea sampling.
TikTok HotspotMatchaGreen TeaPhotosFree Entry
💡 Visit 9–10 AM when the fields are misty and crowd-free. The matcha opera cake (₩7,500) pairs perfectly with an afternoon photo session.
⛰
Hallasan Mountain Hike
Korea's Highest Peak (1,950m)
🕐 Eorimok trail entrance closes 12 PM💰 Free
Hike to the peak of the dormant volcano at the center of Jeju. The Eorimok trail (5.3km, ~3hr up) passes through a Tolkien-like fairytale forest. In June, rhododendrons may still be blooming near the summit.
HikeNatureVolcanoChallenge
💡 Start by 9 AM from Eorimok trailhead. The trail gates close at 12 PM — arrivals after this cannot summit. Pack water and snacks.
🌸
Camellia Hill (카멜리아힐)
Dreamlike Botanical Garden
🔥 TikTok Trending
🕐 8:30 AM – 7 PM (Jun)💰 ₩9,000 (~€6)
An enchanting botanical garden with 500+ camellia varieties, hidden ponds, European-style gardens and red brick pathways. Very Instagram and TikTok popular — has been featured in countless K-drama shoots.
TikTok HotspotGardenRomanticK-DramaPhotos
💡 Early morning visit (8:30–10 AM) before tour buses. The fog over the pond at dawn is magical.
🌊
Hamdeok Beach (함덕 해수욕장)
Trending Transparent Water Beach
🔥 TikTok Trending
🕐 Open 24/7💰 Free
The most photogenic beach in Jeju — transparent jade-green water with black volcanic rocks and lush green hills as backdrop. Less crowded than Haeundae. Very popular on TikTok for the 'natural pool' aesthetic.
TikTok HotspotBeachTransparent WaterSnorkelSunrise
💡 Visit at 7–8 AM for perfectly still turquoise water with zero crowd. Bring snorkeling gear — the water is crystal clear.

Heukdwaeji BBQ (흑돼지)
Daily 5 PM – 11 PM · Black Pork Street near Jeju City
Must Order: Jeju Black Pork Belly BBQ
₩20,000–30,000 per person · €15–22
Jeju's black-coated native pig has a completely different flavour from mainland pork — sweeter, more marbled, richer. Grilled over charcoal tableside. The Heukdwaeji street near Jeju City has 20+ restaurants all specializing in this. This is THE Jeju meal.
🎵 TikTok Viral
🍦
Udo Peanut Ice Cream (땅콩 아이스크림)
Daily 9 AM – 6 PM on Udo Island
Must Order: Peanut Soft Serve on Udo Island
₩2,500 per cone · €2
The single most TikTok-viral food item from Jeju. Udo Island is famous for growing peanuts, and every shop sells peanut soft serve ice cream. Creamy, nutty, different from anything you've had. You MUST try this on the Udo Island day trip.
🎵 TikTok Viral
🍊
Hallabong Juice & Desserts (한라봉)
Shops open daily 9 AM – 9 PM
Must Order: Jeju's Signature Citrus
₩3,000–5,000 · €2–4
Hallabong is Jeju's signature bumpy-orange hybrid citrus — sweeter and more complex than regular oranges. Fresh-squeezed juice, hallabong cheesecake and hallabong sorbet are everywhere. The souvenir jelly boxes (₩8,000–12,000) make great gifts.
🎵 TikTok Viral
🫙
Abalone Porridge (전복죽)
Daily 7 AM – 3 PM (breakfast/lunch specialty)
Must Order: Creamy Abalone Rice Porridge
₩13,000–18,000 · €10–13
Jeju's signature breakfast dish: slow-cooked rice in a rich stock with whole abalone (전복). Served with a small selection of side dishes. The haenyeo women divers harvest the abalone fresh each morning. Find it on Seogwipo's seafood restaurant row.
🎵 TikTok Viral
🍵
O'Sulloc Matcha Soft Serve
Daily 9 AM – 7 PM at O'Sulloc Tea Museum
Must Order: Matcha Soft Serve Ice Cream
₩5,500 · €4
Bright green matcha soft serve made from Jeju green tea. Served at the O'Sulloc Tea Museum surrounded by the green tea fields. The most photogenic dessert in Jeju. Combine with the free museum visit and tea field walk.
🎵 TikTok Viral
🐟
Okdom Grilled Fish (옥돔구이)
Daily lunch & dinner at Seogwipo seafood restaurants
Must Order: Jeju's Signature Grilled Fish
₩25,000–35,000 per fish · €18–26
A flat white fish unique to Jeju's waters, salted and grilled until golden. Served whole with seaweed soup, rice and 10+ side dishes. The fish has delicate, slightly salty flavour. Seogwipo city has the best okdom restaurants.
