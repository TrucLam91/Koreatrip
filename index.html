Bindaetteok
빈대떡 · Mung Bean Pancakes
📍 Gwangjang Market main street — stalls grinding mung beans live in stone mortar
🕐 Daily 8 AM – 9 PM
🥞 Bindaetteok 빈대떡
Mung beans stone-ground fresh on-site, mixed with minced pork, bean sprouts and spring onion, fried in cast iron. Crispy outside, soft inside, nutty flavor. Eat with soy-marinated onion to cut richness.
₩5,000/piece
💡 Choose stalls where you can watch the mung beans being ground live — that means the batter is freshly made, not pre-mixed. Eat immediately while hot and crispy.

🌊Pro Tip: Market + Han River Combo
The Perfect Mangwon Evening 🌅
Young Seoul locals have a classic combo: 1) Shop at Mangwon Market → buy dakgangjeong box (₩9,000), tteokbokki + twigim (₩6,000), kwabekki (₩2,000), drinks. 2) Mangwon Hangang Park (5 min walk) → rent a bicycle (₩3,000/hr) or find a spot on the grass. 3) Watch the Han River sunset with your market haul. Total spend: ₩20,000–30,000 for 2 people for the whole evening. This is the most authentic Seoul couple date experience you can have.
💡 Go on a Friday evening for the most lively atmosphere. Sunset over the Han River from Mangwon Park is one of Seoul's best free views.
